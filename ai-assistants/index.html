FAQ

Questions before you start.

How do you stop the assistant making things up?
It answers from the content it retrieves, and every citation is checked against those sources before the answer is shown, so a claim without a source does not reach your users.
How much does an assistant cost?
An assistant on one content source starts from €2,400 and takes two to four weeks, including the web widget, the admin panel and 30 days of support. One with several sources, single sign-on, usage analytics and permission filtering starts from €4,500.
Where does our data go?
Personal data is stripped before it reaches any third-party model. The assistant can run on OpenAI, Anthropic or open-weight models hosted in your own infrastructure, chosen to fit your privacy requirements.
How does fixed pricing actually work?
Send a brief and you get back a one-page scope covering deliverables, price and timeline. Work starts on a 50% deposit and the balance is due on delivery. Anything outside that written scope is quoted separately before it is built, so the final invoice matches the number you agreed.

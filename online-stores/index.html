FAQ

Questions before you start.

How much does an online store cost?
An online store starts from €4,800 and usually goes live in four to eight weeks. The price depends on the size of your catalogue, the payment and shipping providers you need and how many systems the store connects to, and it is fixed in writing before work starts.
Do you use a hosted platform or build a custom store?
Whichever fits. A hosted platform is quicker to launch and cheaper to run for most catalogues; a custom storefront on your own backend makes sense when you need search, pricing or integrations a platform cannot handle. The proposal says which we recommend, and why.
Can the store connect to our existing systems?
Yes. Inventory and orders can sync with the ERP or stock system you already run, and payments, tax and shipping are integrated with the providers you choose.
How does fixed pricing actually work?
Send a brief and you get back a one-page scope covering deliverables, price and timeline. Work starts on a 50% deposit and the balance is due on delivery. Anything outside that written scope is quoted separately before it is built, so the final invoice matches the number you agreed.

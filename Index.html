    FAQ

    Before you get in touch.

    The questions EU and US teams usually raise before a first engagement.

    How does fixed pricing actually work? +
    Send a brief and you get back a one-page scope covering deliverables, price and timeline. Work starts on a 50% deposit and the balance is due on delivery. Anything outside that written scope is quoted separately before it's built, so the final invoice matches the number you agreed.
    Can you work inside our existing team and codebase? +
    Yes — most engagements happen inside a client's repository, following their branching model, conventions and review process. If those foundations don't exist yet, setting them up is exactly what the deployment package covers.
    Which AI models do you use, and who owns the result? +
    Assistants are built provider-agnostic — OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-weight models hosted in your own infrastructure — chosen against your data-privacy requirements and budget. You own the code, prompts and pipelines outright, and you can switch providers later without a rebuild.
    How do contracts and invoicing work across borders? +
    We invoice as a registered business in Albania, in EUR or USD, by bank transfer or Wise. Contracts can be signed under your jurisdiction's standard contractor agreement, and NDAs are welcome before you share anything.
    What timezone coverage do we get? +
    Tirana is CET (UTC+1): a full working-day overlap with EU teams and roughly 09:00–13:00 Eastern with the US. Retainer clients get a guaranteed same-day response during CET business hours.
    Will you work on an AI system somebody else built? +
    That's most of what the "prove it" services are for. The security review, the quality measurement set and the cost audit all run against a system already in production, whoever wrote it. We don't need to have built it, and we won't propose a rebuild unless the findings genuinely call for one.
    How do you handle GDPR and the EU AI Act? +
    Personal data is stripped before it reaches any third-party model and before it reaches your logs — the second one is where most teams leak without realising. You get a data-flow diagram showing exactly what leaves your systems, what's removed first, and how a deletion request reaches every store. We're engineers rather than lawyers, so we produce the evidence your DPO needs and let them make the call.
    Can we keep our data out of third-party AI providers entirely? +
    Yes. Open-weight models can run inside your own infrastructure, which removes the third-party processor from the picture completely. It costs more in hardware and operations than an API does, so it's worth doing the arithmetic first — we'll show you both numbers rather than assuming which one you want.
    What if we only need advice, not a build? +
    The €450 search audit and the €700 cost audit both work as standalone engagements, and one-off architecture reviews can be arranged. Plenty of clients start there, take the roadmap to their own team, and come back later — or don't. Both are fine.
    What happens if we want to stop working with you? +
    Everything already lives in your accounts, so there is nothing to migrate or hand over. Retainers cancel with 30 days' notice, and fixed-price projects have no ongoing commitment once the 30 days of support end.

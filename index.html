    Index 05 — FAQ

    Before you ask.

    The questions EU and US teams usually raise before a first engagement.

    How do fixed-price projects work? +
    After you send a brief, we reply with a one-page scope document covering deliverables, price, and timeline. Work begins on a 50% deposit; the balance is due on delivery. Anything outside the written scope is quoted separately before it's built — our invoices never surprise you.
    Can you work inside our existing team and codebase? +
    Yes — most of our engagements happen inside an existing repository, following your conventions, branching model, and review process. For teams without those foundations in place, setting them up is exactly what our DevOps foundation package covers.
    Which AI models do you use, and who owns the result? +
    We build assistants provider-agnostic — OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-weight models hosted in your own infrastructure, depending on your data-privacy requirements and budget. You own all code, prompts, and pipelines outright; nothing is licensed back to us.
    How do invoicing and contracts work from Albania? +
    We invoice as a registered business in Albania, in EUR or USD, via standard bank transfer or Wise. Contracts can be signed under your jurisdiction's standard contractor agreement, and NDAs are welcome.
    What timezone coverage can we expect? +
    Tirana is CET (UTC+1): a full working-day overlap with EU teams and roughly 9:00–13:00 overlap with US Eastern mornings. Our retainer clients get guaranteed same-day responses during CET business hours.
    What if we just need advice, not a build? +
    Our €450 Elasticsearch audit works as a standalone advisory engagement, and one-off architecture consultations can be arranged. Many clients start there and move to a fixed-price build once the roadmap is clear.

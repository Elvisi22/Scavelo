    Before you get in touch.

    The questions EU and US teams usually raise before a first engagement.

    How does fixed pricing actually work?
    Send a brief and you get back a one-page scope covering deliverables, price and timeline. Work starts on a 50% deposit and the balance is due on delivery. Anything outside that written scope is quoted separately before it is built, so the final invoice matches the number you agreed.
    Can you work inside our existing team and codebase?
    Yes. Most engagements happen inside a client's repository, following their branching model, conventions and review process. If those foundations do not exist yet, setting them up is exactly what the deployment package covers.
    Which AI models do you use, and who owns the result?
    Assistants are built provider-agnostic: OpenAI, Anthropic or open-weight models hosted in your own infrastructure, chosen against your data-privacy requirements and budget. You own the code, prompts and pipelines outright and can switch providers later without a rebuild.
    How do contracts and invoicing work across borders?
    Invoices are issued in EUR or USD and paid by bank transfer or Wise. Contracts can be signed under your jurisdiction's standard contractor agreement, and NDAs are welcome before you share anything.
    What time-zone coverage do we get?
    Tirana is on Central European Time (UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer): a full working-day overlap with EU teams and roughly 09:00–13:00 Eastern with the US. Retainer clients get a guaranteed same-day response during CET business hours.
    Will you review an AI system somebody else built?
    That is what the review service is for. The security review, the quality test set and the cost audit all run against a system already in production, whoever wrote it. We will not propose a rebuild unless the findings call for one.
    How do you handle GDPR?
    Personal data is stripped before it reaches any third-party model and before it reaches your logs, which is where most teams leak without realising. You get a data-flow diagram showing exactly what leaves your systems, what is removed first, and how a deletion request reaches every store. We are engineers rather than lawyers, so we produce the evidence your DPO needs and let them make the call.
    What happens if we want to stop working with you?
    Everything already lives in your accounts, so there is nothing to migrate or hand over. Retainers cancel with 30 days' notice, and fixed-price projects have no ongoing commitment once the 30 days of support end.

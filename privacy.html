Who we are

Scavelo is an engineering studio based in Tirana, Albania. It is not yet registered as a company; until it is, the data controller for this website is the person who runs it, [your full name]. You can reach us at [contact email].

What we collect and why

  • Briefs sent through the contact form: your name, work email, company, phone number if you choose to give one, and the text of your brief. We use these only to reply to you and prepare a proposal. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to an enquiry you initiated (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) and, where a contract follows, performance of that contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Email you send us: handled the same way as a form submission.
  • Technical data: this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. If you switch the site's language, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the next page opens in the same language; it never leaves your device. Our hosting provider may log IP addresses for security purposes under its own terms.

What you have to give us

Nothing is required to browse this site. To answer an enquiry we need your name, work email, the service you are interested in and a short brief; without them we cannot reply. Your company and phone number are optional.

Who processes your data

  • Formspree, Inc. (United States) — receives form submissions and forwards them to our mailbox.
  • Our email provider — stores our correspondence.
  • GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. (United States) — serves this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts — the page loads typefaces from Google's servers, which receive your IP address in the request.

How long we keep it

Briefs that do not lead to an engagement are deleted 12 months after our last exchange. Data connected to a contract is kept for the retention period required by Albanian accounting law.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to stop processing it, by emailing [contact email]. You can also complain to Albania's Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDP) or, if you are in the EU or UK, to your local supervisory authority.

International transfers

Albania is outside the EU/EEA. Where we work with EU clients, transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in our engagement agreement. Formspree, GitHub and Google are based in the United States and receive data under the safeguards set out in their own terms, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you by automated means, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

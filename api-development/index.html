FAQ

Questions before you start.

Do you work in our repository?
Yes. Work happens in your repository from the first commit, following your branching model, conventions and review process, so your team reviews every change and owns the code outright.
Do you build in Java or TypeScript?
Both. We build in Spring Boot with Java 17 or in NestJS with TypeScript, usually matching what your team already runs so they can maintain it after handover.
What if we need ongoing backend help?
The engineering retainer gives you 25 hours of senior backend capacity a month for €1,500, with same-day responses in CET business hours and a monthly report of everything shipped.
How does fixed pricing actually work?
Send a brief and you get back a one-page scope covering deliverables, price and timeline. Work starts on a 50% deposit and the balance is due on delivery. Anything outside that written scope is quoted separately before it is built, so the final invoice matches the number you agreed.

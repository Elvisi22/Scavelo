FAQ

Questions before you start.

How much does a logo cost?
Logo design starts from €100 and takes one to two weeks. That covers two or three concepts, two rounds of refinement, colour and black-and-white versions, a colour palette, typefaces, one-page brand guidelines and files for web, social and print.
Who owns the logo?
You do. Once the project is paid, the finished logo and its files are yours to use anywhere, with no licence fees and nothing licensed back to us.
Can you design the website as well?
Yes. Many clients start with a logo and move on to a website, and the logo, colours and typefaces carry straight into the site's design. Mention both in your brief and the proposal will cover them together.
How does fixed pricing actually work?
Send a brief and you get back a one-page scope covering deliverables, price and timeline. Work starts on a 50% deposit and the balance is due on delivery. Anything outside that written scope is quoted separately before it is built, so the final invoice matches the number you agreed.

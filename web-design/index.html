FAQ

Questions before you start.

How much does a website cost?
A marketing site starts from €500 and usually goes live in two to four weeks. A web application, such as a customer portal or an internal tool, starts from €3,500 and takes four to eight weeks. Your exact price depends on the pages, integrations and content involved, and it is fixed in writing before work starts.
Can we update the website ourselves?
Yes. Content lives in a headless CMS, so your team edits text, images and pages without a developer or a support ticket. We walk you through it at handover, and the 30 days of support cover any questions after launch.
Will the website show up on Google?
Every site ships with SEO metadata, a sitemap and analytics, loads fast and is built with accessible markup: the technical foundations search engines look for. Rankings also depend on your content and on other sites linking to yours.
How does fixed pricing actually work?
Send a brief and you get back a one-page scope covering deliverables, price and timeline. Work starts on a 50% deposit and the balance is due on delivery. Anything outside that written scope is quoted separately before it is built, so the final invoice matches the number you agreed.
